  • Goal: be the last egg on the map. Fall off = you're out.
  • Punches wind up before they hit — time them. Getting hit cancels your wind-up.
  • Combo: tap punch three times: jab, jab, then a big haymaker that sends eggs flying.
  • Charge: hold punch to power up any punch or weapon swing, then let go. A charged hit smashes through a guard.
  • Flying headbutt: punch while in the air.
  • Block: hold block to stop punches, swings and egg shots from the front — you turn to face the nearest attacker. Only a charged hit smashes through.
  • No juggling: the 4th hit in a quick chain breaks the combo and gives you a moment of safety, and a dazed egg can't be dazed again until it's back up.
  • Grab someone and press punch to throw them.
  • Fell off an edge? Hold grab near the ledge to hang on, then jump (or push forward) to climb up.
  • Weapons (yellow light beams): walk over them. Bat · Frying pan · Mega glove · Egg blaster · Hammer (slow, huge hit) · Bombs (punch to throw, they blow up) · Banana (punch to drop a peel — anyone who steps on it slips) · Rolling pin (big sideways shove) · Spatula (flips eggs up high) · Sausage (fast little slaps).
  • Hot pepper (orange beam): 9 seconds of extra speed and harder hits.
  • Shell broken? You're a yolk — fast but fragile. Grab a green-glowing spare shell to become an egg again.
  • Endless mode (lobby → Mode): team up against wave after wave of bad eggs, with a big boss every 5th wave. Everyone gets 3 lives, and beating a boss gives an extra one. A red ring warns where the next bad egg drops in. The run saves at every wave (or Save run in the pause menu) — pick it up with Continue in the lobby, also with friends online.
  • Watch the maps: trains, bridges, wrecking balls, rolling pins, hot stoves, wind gusts and spinning tables!
  • Air raid: after 40 seconds of a battle round, bombs start falling on orange rings — faster and faster until someone wins.
  • On the tall maps (Jungle Temple, Cliff Climb, Quarry Pit, Treetop Village, Ski Slope) falling off a level isn't the end: jump at a wall and hold grab to climb back up, or take the stairs and ramps.
  • Online: everyone plays on their own computer with Keyboard 1 (WASD) or a controller — or on a phone.
  • Phone: hold your phone sideways, drag your left thumb anywhere on the left half to move, use the JUMP / PUNCH / GRAB / BLOCK buttons on the right, to pause.
