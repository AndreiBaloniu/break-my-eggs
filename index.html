  • Goal: be the last egg on the map. Fall off = you're out.
  • Punches wind up before they hit — time them. Getting hit cancels your wind-up.
  • Grab someone and press punch to throw them.
  • Fell off an edge? Hold grab near the ledge to hang on, then jump (or push forward) to climb up.
  • Weapons (yellow light beams): walk over them. Bat · Frying pan · Mega glove · Egg blaster · Hammer (slow, huge hit) · Bombs (punch to throw, they blow up) · Banana (punch to drop a peel — anyone who steps on it slips).
  • Hot pepper (orange beam): 9 seconds of extra speed and harder hits.
  • Shell broken? You're a yolk — fast but fragile. Grab a green-glowing spare shell to become an egg again.
  • Watch the maps: trains, bridges, wrecking balls, rolling pins, hot stoves, wind gusts and spinning tables!
  • Online: everyone plays on their own computer with Keyboard 1 (WASD) or a controller — or on a phone.
  • Phone: hold your phone sideways, drag your left thumb anywhere on the left half to move, use the JUMP / PUNCH / GRAB buttons on the right, to pause.
