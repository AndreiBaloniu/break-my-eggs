# Break My Eggs: patch notes

## v1.0.26: Faster Sky Course

### Performance
- **Sky Course runs faster:** the clouds under the course are now light, low-poly puffs. The map draws about half as many triangles, so it's much smoother on phones.
- **Rounder things, lighter:** round decorations on all maps use fewer triangles, keeping the low-poly look.

## v1.0.25: Phone fixes

### Phones
- **Main menu:** fits on small phone screens. The buttons no longer overlap or go off the bottom.
- **Controls:** on a phone, the touch controls come first, and the keyboard table is hidden.
- **iPhone:** the Fullscreen button is hidden, because Safari doesn't support it.
- **Cleaner buttons:** keyboard hints like "(Enter)" and "(Esc)" no longer show on phones.

### Maps
- **Floor Is Lava:** eggs no longer spawn on top of each other on the coffee table.
- **Sky Course:** nobody spawns on the spike trap or in the path of a saw.

### Other
- **Controls help:** lists the Rolling Pin, Spatula and Sausage.

## v1.0.24: Online fixes

### Online
- **Joining fixed:** 3 or more people can join an online room again. Eggs with a photo or a drawing made the player list too big to send, so friends saw an empty lobby. Big messages are now sent in pieces.
- **Your own camera:** online, each screen follows your own egg instead of framing everyone. Once you're out, it shows everyone left.

## v1.0.23: New hats, outfits & weapons

### New hats
- **8 new hats:** Sombrero, Fez, Beret, Hard Hat, Cat Ears (in your egg's color), Unicorn (with a rainbow mane), Knight helmet (with a plume) and Mushroom.

### New outfits
- **6 new outfits:** Bandana, Overalls, Hero Emblem, Lei (a flower garland), Suspenders and Bling (a gold chain with a big pendant).

### New weapons
- **Rolling Pin:** a heavy shove that sends eggs flying sideways.
- **Spatula:** flips eggs high up into the air.
- **Sausage:** quick, light slaps with lots of uses.

## v1.0.22: Egg editor & drawing

### New egg editor
- **Customize:** press it on your lobby card to open a big 3D egg you can spin.
- **Tabs:** Colors, Pattern, Face, Hat, Outfit, Photo and Draw.
- **Colors:** prettier swatches, plus a rainbow swatch that opens a new color picker. Drag in the color square and the rainbow bar, or type a color code.
- **Surprise me:** gives your egg a random look.
- **Option pictures:** the Pattern, Face, Hat and Outfit tabs are scrollable lists with a picture of your egg wearing each option.
- **Better fit:** horns, antennae, the mohawk and the halo no longer sink into the head.
- **New hats:** the Santa, Wizard and Pirate hats are rebuilt. Santa has a floppy tip with a pompom, the Wizard hat bends over at the top, and the Pirate hat is a proper three-cornered hat with a skull.
- **Outfits that fit:** the Hero Mask (now a real eye mask), Belt, Sash, Necklace and Medal sit right on the egg, so they no longer float or trap the arms.
- **Cape and scarf:** the cape wraps round the back, and the scarf is a chunky knit round the neck.
- **Faces:** Angry looks angry instead of sad, the Tongue sticks out properly, and Big Eyes has pupils again.
- **No twitching:** changes apply to your egg when you press Done, not while you edit.

### Draw on your egg
- **Drawing:** in the Draw tab, paint right onto the egg. Pick brush colors and sizes, or use the eraser, undo and clear.
- **Turning:** turn the egg with the arrows under it, or press Spin and drag.
- **Sharing:** your drawing is saved with your egg, and friends see it online.
- **Plain egg:** a button in the Draw tab clears the pattern, photo, face, hat and outfit, so you get a clean shell to draw on.
- **Steady egg:** the egg in the editor no longer spins by itself. Turn it with the arrows or by dragging.
- **Egg color while drawing:** the Draw tab also has the egg color picker, so you can change the shell under your drawing.
- **Photo faces:** a photo egg always uses the picture's own face. The cartoon face no longer draws over it.

### Photo eggs
- **Wraps around:** the picture now covers the whole egg. The middle of the picture is the front and the rest wraps round the back.
- **No shine:** the glossy shine no longer covers photo eggs.
- **Face:** the cartoon face switches off when you add a photo, so the picture's face shows. Pick one again under Face if you want it.

### Lobby
- **Cards:** new player cards with a picture of each egg, plus Customize and Random buttons.
- **Buttons:** chunkier lobby buttons.

## v1.0.15: Upside-down maps

### Vertical maps, flipped
- **Start at the top:** on all five vertical maps everyone now starts on the highest, widest level.
- **Smaller going down:** each level below is narrower, so the further you get knocked, the less room you have. Water or a long drop waits at the bottom.
- **Climbing back up:** still works everywhere, with ledge grabs, stairs and ramps.
- **Jungle Temple:** an upside-down pyramid. The boulder rolls all the way down into the river.
- **Cliff Climb:** a wide summit with narrowing ledges down to the lake.
- **Quarry Pit:** you start on the rim.
- **Treetop Village:** a big canopy deck on top, and the swinging log sweeps across it.
- **Ski Slope:** the slope narrows down to an icy lake. A plank bridge leads to the chairlift for the ride back up.

## v1.0.14: Photo eggs

### Customize your egg
- **Any color:** tap the rainbow swatch to pick any shell or pattern color.
- **Photo eggs:** choose the **Photo** pattern, then pick a picture. Drag and zoom it so the face sits on the front of your egg.
- **Cartoon style:** turns the picture into flat toon colors so it fits the game.
- **No face:** the new **None** face hides the cartoon eyes and mouth. It's picked for you when you add a photo.
- Photo eggs show up for your friends online too.

### Fixes
- **Walls:** you can no longer walk through the hut walls in Treetop Village.
- **Decorations:** big decorations are solid now, including candelabras, books, rocks, pumpkins, dirt piles, the reindeer and the sled.

## v1.0.13: Phones, new maps, crowns

### Play on your phone
- **Android app:** download `Break-My-Eggs.apk` from the "Get the game" screen.
- **iPhone app:** `Break-My-Eggs.ipa`, installed from a computer with Sideloadly or AltStore. With a free Apple ID it must be re-installed every 7 days.
- **No install needed:** add the game to your Home Screen from Safari or Chrome. The "Get the game" screen has step-by-step instructions.
- Tapping fast no longer zooms the page on phones.
- New app icon.

### New maps
- Five new multi-level maps where you can climb back up after getting knocked down:
- **Jungle Temple:** a pyramid with a boulder rolling down the stairs.
- **Cliff Climb:** terraces, ramps, a waterfall and rockslides.
- **Quarry Pit:** rock ledges and a runaway mine cart.
- **Treetop Village:** treehouse decks, rope bridges and a swinging log.
- **Ski Slope:** a working chairlift and rolling snowballs.
- More details on maps that felt empty.

### Fighting
- **Block:** hold `I` / `'` / LB to stop punches and egg shots from the front.
- **Combo breaker:** after 4 hits in a row, you get a moment of safety.
- **Air raid:** rounds that drag on get bombed. Watch for the orange rings.
- **Bonk:** items now bonk eggs, with a bump and stars. The frying pan has a proper *clang*.
- Wins now show as one crown with a number, so they don't look like extra health.

### Endless mode
- **Saving:** save your run from the menu, then Continue from the lobby. It also saves at every wave.
- **Enemies:** smarter bots that don't get stuck. They shatter right away and can't use items.
- **Spawns:** markers show where enemies will appear, plus a counter of enemies left.

### Look & feel
- **Eggs:** new animations, including looking around, stretching, waving, teetering on ledges and a weapon flourish.
- **Egg model:** a smaller egg body with longer arms and legs.
- **Visuals:** an even lower-poly look and loading screens between maps.
- **Quitting:** asks you to confirm before quitting the game.
- **What's new:** this pop-up appears after each update. Find it again under Credits.

### Fixes
- **Lag:** smoother frame rate, with fewer frame drops.
- **Strange text:** fixed strange characters in on-screen messages.
- **Pickups:** the frying pan spawns as often as other items, and the chili is the right size.
- **Spawning:** pickups and scenery no longer spawn on top of each other.
- **Moving platforms:** riding them is steadier.
